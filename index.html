SOIL MONITORING UNIT

One unit. Multiple soil parameters.

AgroSentra001 monitors soil temperature, moisture, pH, electrical conductivity, nitrogen, phosphorus, potassium and salinity through an RS485 soil probe. An INA219-based power-monitoring stage reports operating voltage and current, while the integrated LCD provides local readings without requiring the dashboard.
